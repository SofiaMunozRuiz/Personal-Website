About Me

YOUR PHOTO HERE Replace with <img src="images/sofia.jpg">

Sofia Muñoz Ruiz

MSc Student in Neuroengineering · University of Antioquia, Medellín

I am a Physics Engineer and MSc student in Neuroengineering with a strong interdisciplinary background in biomedical signal processing, artificial intelligence, and computational neuroscience. My research focuses on identifying neurophysiological biomarkers associated with preclinical Alzheimer's disease using EEG and deep learning, in collaboration with the Neuroscience Group of Antioquia and Sapienza University of Rome.

My undergraduate thesis — awarded Honorable Mention — studied brain–heart interactions during anesthesia using ML applied to physiological signals from 605 patients (VitalDB database). I also teach laboratory courses in biosignals and AI/ML applied to biomedical engineering at UdeA.

DAAD scholarship recipient. Presented research at the University Medical Center Göttingen and University of Augsburg. Native Spanish · B2.2 English · A2 French.
