About Me

Sofia Muñoz Ruiz

MSc Student · Neuroengineering · University of Antioquia, Medellín

I am a highly skilled professional with a BSc in Engineering Physics and MSc student in Engineering, specializing in Neuroengineering, with a strong interdisciplinary background in biomedical signal processing, artificial intelligence, and computational neuroscience. My academic training has provided me with solid foundations in mathematics, physics, statistics, signal processing, image analysis, and data science using Python and MATLAB. My research experience spans EEG and ECG analysis, brain–heart interactions during anesthesia, machine learning, deep learning, and the study of neurophysiological biomarkers associated with neurological disorders such as Alzheimer’s disease.

Currently, I conduct research in collaboration with the Neuroscience Group of Antioquia and Sapienza University of Rome, focusing on the identification of neurophysiological patterns associated with the preclinical stages of Alzheimer’s disease using EEG signals recorded during serious games. In parallel, I teach laboratory courses in biosignals, artificial intelligence, and machine learning, supervising projects related to Brain–Computer Interfaces (BCIs) and neural signal analysis.

My experience in data analysis within healthcare and neuroscience, combined with strong analytical, research, and collaborative skills, has enabled me to work effectively in multidisciplinary research environments. I am particularly motivated to continue exploring diverse areas of neuroscience and biomedical engineering, applying advanced computational methods to better understand brain function. My long-term goal is to contribute to the development of innovative tools and knowledge that advance our understanding of the human brain.
